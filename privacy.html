Website privacy & cookies.

How we measure website visits, and the choices you have.

Updated 5 October 2026

Website analytics

With your permission, HYA Wellness uses Google Analytics to understand how people find and use this website. This includes pages viewed, referring websites or campaign links, approximate location, browser and device information, and clicks on visit-planning, WhatsApp and brochure links. Browser identifiers help us estimate the number of visitors.

This analytics setup does not send the contents of your email or WhatsApp conversations to Google Analytics. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our website analytics code.

Your choice

Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose Accept analytics. If you reject analytics or leave the choice unanswered, this setup does not send your visit to Google Analytics. You can use the website either way.

Use Cookie settings to change your choice. Rejecting analytics stops future measurement by this setup and removes its analytics cookies from this website; it does not erase data already sent. Your choice is remembered in this browser for up to 180 days using local storage. Clearing browser storage or using another browser may prompt you again.

Cookies and processing

When you accept, Google Analytics can set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_C2YH6YKXZT to distinguish browsers and visits. We configure an expiry of up to 180 days, which may be renewed on later visits; your browser may apply a shorter limit.

Google processes website usage data to provide our analytics reports. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and external services may process the technical information needed to deliver their services under their own policies. Links to WhatsApp and other websites are governed by those services' privacy policies.

Questions

For questions about HYA's website analytics or your choices, contact sales@hyacollective.com.
