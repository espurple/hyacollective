About HYA Wellness

A bridge to authentic wellbeing.

China holds some of the world’s most advanced preventive medicine and its deepest living wellness traditions. HYA Wellness exists to make both genuinely accessible to international guests — without the friction, the guesswork or the language barrier.

Our philosophy

Prevention and restoration, as one journey.

We believe true wellbeing is both clear-eyed and restorative — understanding your body, and caring for it. So we bring advanced preventive screening together with the balance of Traditional Chinese Medicine and the calm of Chinese cultural practice, into a single, unhurried experience.

And we design it the way an international guest would actually want it: private, comfortable, paced gently, and supported in English from the first message to the final report.

How we work

A curator and host, working through a trusted network.

HYA Wellness designs, curates and hosts your experience. The medical, screening and treatment services are provided by a vetted network of licensed hospitals, screening centres and practitioners in Shanghai. We are an experience curator — not a medical provider or a travel agency — and that clarity is part of how we keep every journey safe, honest and well-run.
