The Journey

Inspiration first. The logistics disappear.

You bring the curiosity. We handle everything between finding an experience you love and being looked after on the day — so a wellness visit to Shanghai feels effortless from the first message.

Always in English

Fully supported — two ways.

Some of our experiences are led directly in English. For others, a Chinese-speaking physician, practitioner or instructor leads while a dedicated English coordinator stays alongside you — translating, explaining and making sure nothing is missed. Either way, you’re never left guessing.

Our role

The curator and host — not the clinic.

HYA Wellness designs, curates and hosts your experience. The medical and screening services themselves are provided by a vetted network of licensed hospitals, screening centres and practitioners in Shanghai — they deliver the care; we make it understandable, comfortable and seamless for an international guest.

Being clear about this matters: HYA Wellness is an experience curator, not a medical provider or a travel agency. Any health screening is for your information and direction and is not a diagnosis, and our wellness experiences complement rather than replace conventional medical care.
