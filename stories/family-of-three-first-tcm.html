Chinese medicine · Family visit

A family of three tries Chinese medicine together in Shanghai

Guests
N. and two family members
Age
Prefers not to disclose
Visiting from
Philippines
Main concerns
Different concerns across the family — tension, sleep quality and general energy
Arranged by HYA
Back-to-back English-accessible TCM consultations with tuina and acupuncture

What brought them to us

The family wanted to experience Chinese medicine together while visiting Shanghai. Each of them arrived with something slightly different — tension and stiffness for one, sleep quality for another, and general energy for the third — and they wanted the experience to be shared rather than clinical.

What we arranged

We arranged back-to-back consultations at a partner clinic so the family could stay together throughout. Each received individual pulse and tongue diagnosis, followed by treatment tailored to their own pattern — a combination of acupuncture and tuina — with the physician explaining each step in English as they went.

What happened

What struck the family most was how personal each consultation felt despite being arranged as a group. They left with individual guidance for each person and a shared sense of having understood something new about how Chinese medicine reads the body.

In their words

It felt personal for each of us, even though we came together.

One guest's personal experience, shared with permission. Individual experiences vary and outcomes are not guaranteed. HYA Wellness curates and coordinates; all care is delivered by licensed partner practitioners. Chinese medicine is intended to complement, not replace, conventional medical care.
